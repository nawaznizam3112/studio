# **App Name**: RewardBoost

## Core Features:

- Campaign Setup: Allow clinics to create and customize 'Pay for service -> get free draw entry' campaigns.
- QR Code Check-in: Enable patients to scan a QR code that redirects them to a digital portal, automating entry after payment.
- Automated Winner Selection: Random winner selection process, ensuring transparency and fairness.
- Marketing Material Generation: Generate ready-made posters, WhatsApp campaign text, and SMS reminders.
- Clinic Dashboard: Provide clinics with a dashboard to track entries, engagement, winner lists, and generate potential leads.
- Automated Patient Follow-Up Suggestions: Provide suggestions of past patients to follow up with to fill currently open appointment slots; act as a tool for helping offices efficiently use their resources.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A vibrant teal (#008080) to represent trust, growth, and health, aligning with the medical and marketing aspects of the app.
- Background color: A desaturated light teal (#E0F8F8), offering a clean and calming backdrop.
- Accent color: A muted blue (#4682B4) to highlight calls to action and important information.
- Headline font: 'Poppins' (sans-serif) for a modern, geometric, precise, fashionable feel. Body text: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) due to its warmth and readability, especially in paragraphs.
- Use simple, clear icons that represent different services and actions within the app.
- Clean, card-based layout for easy navigation and overview of campaigns, analytics, and tools.
- Subtle transitions and animations to guide users and provide feedback on interactions, keeping the UI engaging without being distracting.
